AppBlips turns your ideas into working apps, websites and games. You describe what you want in everyday words, an AI writes the code, and the result appears in a live preview a moment later. You don't need to know how to code. When you're happy with it, download it as files you own, or publish it to the web.

AppBlips is free and open source. It runs on your own computer (or in your browser), and it works with the AI provider you choose.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your idea">
    Pick what you want to make (an app, a website or a game) and describe it in plain English. For example: "a habit tracker with a checkbox for each day and a weekly progress bar".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it build">
    The AI writes the code while you watch. When it's done, your project appears in a live preview that you can click, type into and play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    Ask for changes in the chat, or click text and images in a website to edit them yourself. Every change is saved as a version, so you can always go back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it with you">
    Download your project as an HTML file, a `.zip` of pages or a React project, or publish it to a free web address.
  </Step>
</Steps>
