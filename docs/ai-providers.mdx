AppBlips uses an AI to write your code. That AI comes from an **AI provider**, a company such as Anthropic or OpenAI. You connect to one once, and every build and edit goes through it. Some copies of AppBlips also include [free AI](#free-ai).

## Key terms

- **API key:** a password-like code from your provider. It lets AppBlips use the AI on your account, and the provider bills that account. Keep it private.
- **Model:** a specific AI from that provider. Larger ones write better code but cost more and run slower.
- **Token:** the unit providers charge by. One token is about three-quarters of a word. A typical build uses tens of thousands.

AppBlips is free. With your own key, you only pay your provider for the tokens you use. Many providers need a payment method or prepaid credit before a new key works.

## Supported providers

- **Anthropic** (Claude), marked recommended in AppBlips
- **OpenAI** (GPT), using a model that supports tool calling
- **Gemini** (Google)
- **xAI** (Grok)
- **DeepSeek**
- **OpenRouter**, one key for models from many companies

## Get an API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your provider's key page">
    Sign in or sign up, then open the API keys page:

    - Anthropic: [platform.claude.com/settings/keys](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys)
    - OpenAI: [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys)
    - Gemini: [aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey)
    - xAI: [console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai/team/default/api-keys)
    - DeepSeek: [platform.deepseek.com/api_keys](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys)
    - OpenRouter: [openrouter.ai/settings/keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys)

    During setup, **Get a key** opens the right page for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy a key">
    Create a key and name it something like "AppBlips". Copy it right away, because many providers show it only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add billing if asked">
    If the provider needs a payment method or credit, add it in the same website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect it in AppBlips

You can do this during setup on **Choose the AI that builds with you.**, or any time in Settings:

1. Click **Settings** and open the **AI** tab.
2. Under **AI coding provider**, choose your **Provider**.
3. Paste your API key.
4. Choose a **Model**. The list loads from your provider once your key is in. If it doesn't, click **Refresh models**, or type the model's exact name from your provider's website.
5. Click **Test connection**.
6. Click **Save**.

To switch, choose another provider and save. To remove your provider and key, click **Clear**.

## Where your key is kept

- **Desktop app:** encrypted by your computer's keychain and kept on your computer. On Linux without a keychain, the protection is weak, so install GNOME Keyring or KWallet. After saving, AppBlips can't show the key again. To change it, enter a new one and save.
- **Browser version** (web.appblips.com, Pinokio, source, Docker): saved in your browser and sent with each request so it can reach your provider. The server doesn't store it. The browser keeps it unencrypted, so anyone who can use your browser profile on this computer could read it.
- **Android app:** kept in the Android Keystore.

Either way, your key never goes into your projects or anything you export or publish.

## Free AI

Some copies, such as [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com), come with AI set up by whoever runs them. There you'll see **AI is included, free.** during setup and **Free (no key needed)** in the provider list.

- You don't choose a model. The host picks and pays for it.
- In the browser version, you may need to sign in with Google or GitHub before your first Free request, so limits count each person separately. You can sign in or out in **Settings → AI**.
- It's shared and limited. If you hit a limit, AppBlips says when you can try again.
- Switch to your own key any time in **Settings → AI**. AppBlips doesn't limit your own key.

Running your own server? You can offer Free AI by giving it a provider and key. See the [Configuration reference](/self-hosting/configuration).

## Pick a model

- **Larger models** suit complex apps and games and follow long instructions better.
- **Smaller models** are cheaper and quicker, and fine for simple pages.
- **To build from screenshots,** choose a model that can read images.

Not sure? Start with your provider's current general-purpose model. You can change it at any time, even mid-project.

## Reasoning

Many models can think before they write. **Reasoning** in **Settings → AI** sets how much. Higher suits complex projects but is slower and costs more. Lower is faster and cheaper. The list shows only the levels your model supports and marks its usual one as **Default**.

## More AI settings

In **Settings → AI** you can also change:

- **Output tokens: building:** the longest reply the AI may write when building or editing. Leave it empty for the default of 64,000. Raise it only if big projects stop early and your model allows more.
- **Output tokens: Ask:** the longest reply in Ask mode. Empty lets your provider decide.
- **Clarifying questions:** the AI asks 2–3 multiple-choice questions before the first build.
- **Suggested prompts:** shows ideas for your next prompt after each build.

If the **Output tokens** fields are missing, the host of your copy sets the limit for everyone. See the [Settings reference](/reference/settings) for every setting.

## Web search

The AI only knows what it learned in training. To let it look up current facts, such as opening hours:

- **Your provider's search.** Each provider has a web search tool in **Settings → Tools**. The one matching your provider turns on automatically and adds a globe button to the chat box. Click the globe to turn search off or on. Your provider charges for each search on top of normal tokens. See [Tools](/features/tools#web-search-with-your-ai-provider) for details.
- **Tavily.** With any provider, add a free [Tavily](/features/connectors#tavily) key in **Settings → Extensions**. When it's connected, it's used instead.
