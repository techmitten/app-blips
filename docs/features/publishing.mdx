**Publish** puts your project online so anyone can open it. AppBlips uses [Surge](https://surge.sh), a free hosting service, and gives you an address such as `https://my-bakery.surge.sh`. It always uses HTTPS, the padlock that keeps the connection secure.

Publishing works for apps, websites and games. Imported React + Vite sites can't be published; [export them](/features/importing-sites#export-the-project) instead.

## Publish for the first time

You need a free Surge account. You can create one inside AppBlips.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Publish window">
    In the preview toolbar, click **Publish** (the globe at the right end).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Surge">
    New to Surge? Click **Create an account**, enter your email and a password, type it again under **Type the password again**, then click **Create account**.

    Already have one? Keep **I have an account** selected, enter your email and password, then click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the web address">
    AppBlips suggests a name. Keep it or type your own, with lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. The address ends in `.surge.sh`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick any options">
    All are optional, and AppBlips remembers them for each project:

    - **Add icon…:** the small picture in browser tabs and bookmarks. A square image works best. If you don't add one, your site shows the AppBlips icon (unless the page already sets its own).
    - **Let search engines find this site:** on by default. See [Search engines](#search-engines).
    - **Prompt visitors to install as an app:** invites visitors to add your site to their phone or computer like an app.
    - **Password protect this site:** see [Password protection](#password-protection).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. After a few seconds you'll see **Your site is live**. Click **Copy** to copy the link, or **Open site** to visit it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Unless you add a password, anyone with the link can see your site. Don't publish anything private.
</Note>

Under **Share it**, click an icon to share the link by email or on X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, Bluesky or WhatsApp. Nothing is posted until you post it yourself. To feature your project in the Showcase, click **Submit it to the showcase**. See [Showcase](/features/showcase).

## Update a published site

After you change your project, click **Update** (the button reads **Publish** until the project has been published). The window says **This project is online**.

- **Update site:** replaces what's online with your latest version, at the same address. The icon, search, install and password options can be changed here too.
- **Publish to a different address:** puts a second copy online at a new address. The old one stays up until you unpublish it.
- **Unpublish:** takes the site offline.

New versions can take a minute to appear everywhere.

## Use your own domain

If you own a domain such as `mybakery.com`, you can publish to it instead. Surge hosts custom domains for free and adds HTTPS for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to your domain">
    In the Publish window, click **Use a domain I own** next to **Web address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter it and publish">
    Type your domain, such as `www.mybakery.com`, then click **Publish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS record">
    The window shows one record to add. Add it in the DNS settings at the company where you bought the domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Your address | Type | Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `www.mybakery.com` | CNAME | `www` | `geo.surge.world` |
| `mybakery.com` | A | `@` | `138.197.235.123` |

Not sure where to add it? Click **Not sure how? Guides for GoDaddy, Namecheap and other providers** under the record and pick your company.

Custom domains are in beta. Two things can hold you up:

- **Cloudflare:** if your DNS is at Cloudflare, turn the orange cloud beside the record grey (**DNS only**). The window tells you when this is the problem.
- **Surge email:** Surge holds a custom domain until you verify your account. Click the link in Surge's email. The window can resend it.

Once the record works, Surge gets a free HTTPS certificate on its own, usually within a few minutes.

The window checks your DNS every 30 seconds while it's open. Click **Check now** to check straight away. When it works, it says **Your site is live**. DNS changes can take up to a day, so your domain may not open the site until then. Come back later and click **Update** to see **Waiting for DNS** or **Live**.

To go back to a free address, click **Publish to a different address**, then **Use a free .surge.sh address**.

## Search engines

**Let search engines find this site** is on for new sites.

- **Off:** AppBlips asks search engines to leave your site out of results. That's a polite request, not a lock. Anyone with the link can still open the site.
- **On:** your site can appear in search results. If the [SEO tool](/features/tools#seo) is on and has your website address, AppBlips also publishes a `sitemap.xml` to help search engines find your pages.

A password-protected site is always kept out of search results.

## Password protection

Turn on **Password protect this site**, enter a password of at least 10 characters under **Site password**, and repeat it under **Type it again**. Visitors then see a lock screen and need the password to get in.

- The pages are locked on your device before upload. Your password is never uploaded or saved.
- Everything is hidden until the password is entered, including your pictures. Only the site icon is public.
- Visitors enter the password once per browser tab.
- Updating the site asks for the password again, because AppBlips doesn't keep it. Enter the same one, or a new one to change it. To remove the password, turn the switch off and click **Update site**.

<Warning>
  A static host can't lock anyone out after wrong guesses, so someone could try many passwords offline. Use a long password, such as four random words.
</Warning>

## Take a site offline

1. Open the project and click **Update**.
2. Click **Unpublish**.
3. Click **Unpublish** again to confirm, or **Keep it online** to cancel.

The link stops working, and your project stays in AppBlips. Someone else could claim a `.surge.sh` address after you free it.

AppBlips only remembers each project's most recent address. To remove an older one, install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) and run `npx surge teardown old-name.surge.sh` with your own address.

## Your Surge account

- Your password goes to Surge once and is never saved. AppBlips keeps only the sign-in Surge sends back: encrypted with your computer's keychain in the desktop app, or unencrypted in your own browser in the browser version.
- To sign in ahead of time or sign out, go to **Settings → Extensions** and open the **Surge** card. **Sign out** is also in the Publish window.
- You can publish to a `.surge.sh` address without confirming Surge's email, but a custom domain needs it. If you forget your password, reset it at [surge.sh](https://surge.sh).

## Limits

- Surge addresses are first come, first served. If yours is taken, pick another name.
- A custom domain can be on only one Surge account.
- If Surge refuses because you published too often, wait a few minutes.
- Your site's images can total at most 60 MB. Only the [My images](/features/tools#my-images-beta) pictures your pages use are uploaded.
- [Pollinations](/features/connectors#pollinations) images are copied into your site, so they stay as long as it does.
- Surge's [terms](https://surge.sh/terms) apply to what you publish.
