There are two ways to change what AppBlips built:

- **Ask for it in the chat.** Works in every studio. Describe the change and the AI makes it.
- **Click on the page.** Works in the Website studio. Edit text, swap pictures, change links and move things around yourself, with no AI and no waiting.

Every change, whichever way you make it, is saved as a new version. If you don't like it, step back with **Previous version** or Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac).

## Ask for a change in the chat

Type what you want different in the prompt box and send it. Plain words work best:

```
Change the background to dark blue and the text to white.
```

```
Add a search box at the top that filters the list as you type.
```

```
Move the Submit button to the bottom right and make it green.
```

The AI changes the app you have rather than starting again, so you can keep going one request at a time. For more on writing requests, see [Build an app from a prompt](/features/building-apps).

## Edit the page directly (Website studio)

In the Website studio, two keys on the right of the preview toolbar let you work on the page itself:

- **Click-to-edit mode** (a pointer clicking): click things on the page to select and edit them.
- **Drag mode** (four arrows): drag things on the page to move them. Turning it on also turns on click-to-edit.

The key turns red while it's on. Click it again to go back to using your site normally.

With click-to-edit on, hover over the page to see what you'd select, then click. An editor bar opens at the top of the preview with the options for that kind of element. To close it, press Escape or click **Close editor** (the X at its end).

Changes you make this way are applied instantly, without the AI, and each one is saved as a new version.

### Edit text

Click a heading, paragraph or button label and type straight into it. (For a link, double-click it.) A formatting bar appears while you type:

- **Undo** and **Redo** for your typing.
- The font, and the font size: **Smaller**, **Larger**, or type a size in pixels.
- **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline** and **Uppercase**.
- **Align left**, **Align center** and **Align right**.
- **Text color**, from a set of colors or a custom one.

Press Enter to start a new line. To save, click **Done**, click somewhere else on the page, or press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac). To throw your typing away, click **Cancel** or press Escape.

### Change a picture

Click a picture to change it in the editor bar:

- **Image URL:** paste the web address of a different picture.
- **Upload:** replace it with a picture from your computer (up to 8 MB).
- **Fit size to image:** appears after you upload. Tick it to keep the picture's width and change its height to match the new picture's shape.
- **Alt text** (a captions icon): a short description of the picture for people who use screen readers. It also shows if the picture can't load.

### Change links, colors and backgrounds

Depending on what you selected, the editor bar also offers:

- **Link:** type a web address, or pick one of your site's pages or a `#section` from the suggestions. The arrow key beside it switches between opening the link in the same tab and in a new tab.
- **Fill:** a background color for sections, buttons and links. If the element had a gradient, a solid color replaces it.
- **Wallpaper:** upload a picture as a section's background, whether or not it had one before.

### Select the section around something

Sometimes you want the box around an element, such as a card or a whole section, rather than the element itself. Select the element, then click the **Select parent** key in the editor bar (an arrow pointing up to a line; its tooltip says "Select the parent"). Click it again to go out another level, until the part you want is outlined.

### Move, copy and delete

The editor bar has keys to rearrange what you selected:

- **Move up** and **Move down** among its neighbors: Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ (⌥↑ and ⌥↓ on a Mac).
- **Duplicate** it, right below itself: Ctrl+D (⌘D on a Mac).
- **Delete** it: Delete or Backspace.

A duplicate is an exact copy, except that it doesn't copy `id` attributes, so links to the original section still go to the original.

### Drag to move

With **Drag mode** on, press on any text, picture, button or section and drag it to a new spot:

1. Press on the element and start dragging. A copy of it follows your pointer (big elements shrink so you can still see the page).
2. A line shows where it will land, before or after the element under your pointer.
3. Let go to drop it there, or press Escape to cancel.

Hold the pointer near the top or bottom edge of the preview to scroll while you drag.

In drag mode, a click selects an element without editing its text. To move a container, such as a card or a section, click it (use **Select parent** if needed), then drag from anywhere inside it. Turn drag mode off to edit text again.

Things you move stay part of the page's normal layout, so the page still fits every screen size. A few rules keep the page sensible: words move with their paragraph, list items only move among list items, and table cells only among table cells.

### Select several things at once

To change or delete several elements together:

- **Drag a box** across the page. Everything fully inside it is selected. A box around three cards selects the three cards, not the headings inside them.
- **Shift+click** elements to add them one at a time. Shift+click a selected one to take it out.
- **Shift+drag** a box to add its elements to what you already have.

In the phone and tablet views, dragging scrolls the page as a finger would, so hold Shift to draw a box there. You can't draw a box in drag mode.

The editor bar then shows how many are selected (up to 40). Describe a change and click **Apply with AI** to have the AI change all of them, or click the trash key (or press Delete) to remove them all. Press Escape or click **Clear selection** to stop.

## The right-click menu

With click-to-edit on, right-click anywhere on the page. A blue line shows where anything you add or paste will go, and a menu opens.

If you right-clicked some text or a picture, it gets a dashed outline, and the top of the menu acts on it:

- **Cut:** removes it and keeps a copy to paste.
- **Copy:** keeps a copy to paste and leaves it where it is.
- **Duplicate:** adds a copy right below it.
- **Move:** turns on drag mode so you can drag it somewhere else.
- **Delete:** removes it.

These are always in the menu:

- **Paste:** puts the last thing you cut or copied at the blue line. You can paste on any page of the same project, as many times as you like.
- **Ask AI:** describe a change, such as "make it bigger" or "add a sign-up button here", then click **Ask AI** or press Enter. The AI is told exactly where you right-clicked, so the change lands in the right spot.
- **Add text container:** type some text (Shift+Enter starts a new line), then click **Add** or press Enter. It's added as a new paragraph.
- **Add image:** pick a picture from your computer. It's added to the project's [My images](/features/tools#my-images-beta) and placed at the line.

Cut and Copy work inside AppBlips only. They don't use your computer's clipboard. To close the menu without changing anything, press Escape or click elsewhere.

If the menu says `Nothing can be added or pasted at this spot. Right-click next to some text or an image.`, try right-clicking closer to some text or a picture.

## Ask the AI about one element

When a change is more than a quick tweak, select the element and click **Ask AI** in the editor bar. Describe the change, then:

- **Apply with AI** makes it straight away, as a new version.
- **Add to chat** puts the element and your request into the prompt box, so you can add more before you send it.

The AI gets the element's exact code and where it sits on the page, so it changes that element and not a look-alike somewhere else.

### When a direct edit isn't possible

Some parts of a page are created by the page's own code while it runs, so they can't be found in the page's source. If you try to edit, move or delete one of those, nothing changes. The editor bar tells you why and switches to **Ask AI**, so you can describe the change instead.

## Edit the code yourself

If you know some HTML, CSS or JavaScript, open the **Code** tab, click **Edit**, make your change and click **Save**. See [Edit the code yourself](/features/live-preview#edit-the-code-yourself).

## Undo any change

Every change is saved as a version:

- Click **Previous version** and **Next version** in the preview toolbar.
- Or press Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo (⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on a Mac). This works even while you're editing on the page.

To jump straight to an older version, open the [history drawer](/features/live-preview#the-history-drawer).

<Tip>
  Mix both ways of editing. Click on the page for quick fixes to wording, pictures and order, and use the chat for bigger changes like new features or a new layout.
</Tip>
