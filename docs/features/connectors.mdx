Extensions connect AppBlips to outside services. Once you connect one, the AI uses it whenever it builds or changes a project, without you having to mention it. Ask for "a contact form" and it emails you; ask for "a buy button" and it opens your checkout.

You find them under **Settings → Extensions**. Each service has a card that shows whether it's set up:

- **Connected:** it's on and ready, and the AI uses it.
- **Needs key** or **Needs sign-in:** it's on, but AppBlips still needs something from you.
- **Not connected:** it's off.

Looking for SEO or My images? They're built into AppBlips instead of being outside services, so they're under **Settings → Tools**. See [Tools](/features/tools).

<Note>
  AppBlips has no partnership, sponsorship or affiliate deal with any of these services. They're offered because they work without you running a server. Each one is a separate company with its own terms, prices and privacy policy, and questions about your account go to them. Their names and logos belong to their owners.
</Note>

## What each extension does

- **[Web3Forms](#web3forms):** forms on your site email their entries to you.
- **[Umami](#umami)** and **[Plausible](#plausible):** count your visitors.
- **[tawk.to](#tawkto):** a live chat bubble so visitors can message you.
- **[Square](#square):** buy, pay and donate buttons that take real payments.
- **[Pollinations](#pollinations):** AI-generated images, and AI features such as a chatbot.
- **[Google Sheets Backend](#google-sheets-backend) (beta):** saves RSVPs, sign-ups, votes and scores in your own Google Sheet.
- **[Pixabay](#pixabay):** search free stock photos and add them to your project.
- **[Tavily](#tavily):** lets the AI search the web for real facts while it builds.
- **[Surge](#surge):** the free hosting that **Publish** uses.

Not every studio uses every extension:

- **Responsive Website** and **Mobile & Web App** use all of them.
- **Browser Game** uses Umami, Plausible, Pollinations (for title screens, backgrounds and sprites), Google Sheets Backend (for a shared leaderboard) and Tavily. It doesn't use forms, chat or payments.
- **Imported sites** (React + Vite projects) only use Tavily.

## Set up an extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Extensions">
    Click **Settings**, then the **Extensions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Click its card. To read what the service does before you start, click **How it works** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click the switch next to **Enable** and the service's name, for example **Enable Square**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what the service gives you">
    Paste the key, code or link into the box. The section for each service below says where to find it. A green message starting with **Connected.** appears when it looks right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save on their own, so there's no Save button. To go back to the cards, click **Extensions** at the top of the page.

### Add an extension to a project you already built

Projects you built before connecting a service don't change on their own. To add the service to one:

1. Open the project.
2. Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**, then the service's card.
3. Under **Prompts to try**, click **Copy** next to the request you want.
4. Close Settings, paste the request into the chat and send it.

Do the same if you change your key or link later and want an existing project to use the new one.

### What goes into your pages

Most extensions only need something that's meant to be public: a form key, a tracking code, a payment link or a web address. That value is written into your pages and into anything you export or publish, which is how those services are designed to work.

Pollinations, Pixabay, Tavily and Surge are different: they use a sign-in or a personal key. AppBlips keeps those to itself and never writes them into a page:

- In the **desktop app**, they're encrypted with your computer's keychain.
- In the **browser version** (from source, Docker or [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com)), they're saved in your own web browser, not on the server.

## Web3Forms

Web3Forms sends the entries from your forms to your email, so a contact form works without a server. It has a free plan.

1. Go to [web3forms.com](https://web3forms.com) and enter the email address you want entries sent to.
2. Web3Forms emails you an **access key**: a 36-character code that looks like `xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx`.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Web3Forms and paste the key into the **Access key** box.

From then on, contact, signup and booking forms the AI builds send their entries to you. After someone presses send, the form says on the page whether it worked.

<Note>
  The access key is designed to sit in a website's code: it can only send email to your own inbox. Forms work in the preview too, so a test entry there lands in your inbox.
</Note>

## Umami

Umami counts the visitors to your projects and shows them on a dashboard: how many people came, which pages they viewed, and where they came from. It doesn't use cookies, so the AI doesn't add a cookie banner for it. Umami has a free plan, and you can also [run it on your own server](https://github.com/umami-software/umami).

1. Sign up at [umami.is](https://umami.is) and add your website.
2. In Umami, open the website's settings and copy its tracking code.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Umami and paste the code into the **Tracking code or website ID** box. On Umami Cloud you can paste just the 36-character website ID instead.

The AI adds the tracking code to every page. Visits from the AppBlips preview, including the AI's own testing, aren't counted. Your stats start once the project is online.

## Plausible

Plausible counts your visitors and shows them on a dashboard, like Umami. It doesn't use cookies either. Plausible is a paid service with a free trial, and you can also [run it on your own server](https://github.com/plausible/community-edition).

1. Sign up at [plausible.io](https://plausible.io) and add your site, using the web address it will have once it's online.
2. In Plausible, open the site's settings and copy everything in the **Site installation** section.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Plausible and paste it into the **Tracking code** box.

The AI adds the tracking code to every page. Preview visits aren't counted. Stats start once the project is online at the address you gave Plausible.

<Tip>
  For Umami and Plausible, AppBlips keeps only the parts of the tracking code it needs. Anyone who sees the code can add visits to your stats, but can't read them.
</Tip>

## tawk.to

tawk.to adds a live chat bubble to the corner of your pages. Visitors click it to message you, and you reply from the tawk.to dashboard or its phone app. It's free, with optional paid extras.

1. Sign up at [tawk.to](https://www.tawk.to) and create a property for your website.
2. In tawk.to, open **Administration**, then **Chat Widget**.
3. Copy everything in the **Widget Code** box, from `<!--Start of Tawk.to Script-->` to `<!--End of Tawk.to Script-->`.
4. In AppBlips, turn on tawk.to and paste it into the **Widget code** box.

The AI adds the chat bubble to every page and keeps the bottom-right corner clear for it. The chat works in the preview too, so a message you send there reaches your dashboard. tawk.to uses cookies to remember chats, so where the law requires it, your site may need a cookie notice.

## Square

Square takes payments for you. For each thing you sell, you create a **payment link** in Square: a web address that opens Square's checkout page. The buy, order, pay and donate buttons on your site open that link, people pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and the money goes to your Square account. Square charges a fee on each payment.

1. Sign up at [squareup.com](https://squareup.com/payment-links), or sign in to the Square Dashboard if you already use Square.
2. Open **Payment links**. If you can't see it, type "payment links" in the Dashboard's search box.
3. Create a link. Square asks what it's for (a payment, an item, a donation or an event), its name and its price.
4. Click **Copy link**. The link starts with `https://square.link/u/`.
5. In AppBlips, turn on Square and paste the link into the **Payment links** box.

To sell more than one thing, put each link on its own line, with the name of what it sells in front:

```text
T-shirt https://square.link/u/AbC123xy
Mug https://square.link/u/DeF456zw
```

The AI uses the names to put each link on the right button. You can also paste Square's button code, and AppBlips keeps just the link.

Prices live in Square. To change one, edit the payment link in Square; your site doesn't need to change. Payments are real, even from the preview. In the desktop app, the checkout opens in your normal web browser.

<Warning>
  Only paste payment links. AppBlips never needs your Square access token or any other secret, and refuses one if you paste it. If you've shared a token anywhere, replace it in the Square Developer Console.
</Warning>

## Pollinations

[Pollinations](https://github.com/pollinations/pollinations) is an open-source service for AI images and text. With it connected, you can ask for custom images instead of stock photos, and for AI features such as a chatbot, a writing helper or an image generator. Pollinations runs on its own credits, called **Pollen**, and has its own terms and prices.

Using Pollinations through AppBlips costs 25% more than using it directly. The extra supports AppBlips. It applies to images you make, and, if you choose **Visitors use their own Pollen (safer)**, to your visitors' AI use too. The **Connect Pollinations** button in your sites tells visitors: "Includes a small fee that supports AppBlips."

### Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pollinations">
    Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**, then the **Pollinations** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the sign-in">
    Click **Sign in with Pollinations**. A Pollinations page opens in your web browser. If it doesn't, click **open it here** in AppBlips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve AppBlips">
    On the Pollinations page, sign in and approve AppBlips. If it asks for a code, type the one AppBlips shows. This is also where you choose the most Pollen AppBlips may spend and how many days the sign-in lasts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to AppBlips">
    AppBlips shows **Signed in as** your account name, and how much Pollen it has left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click the switch next to **Enable Pollinations**. It won't turn on until you're signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The sign-in ends after the number of days you chose, or once it has spent its Pollen limit. Pollinations then turns off: sign in again and turn the switch back on. To end it sooner, click **Sign out**, or delete it on the Keys page at [enter.pollinations.ai](https://enter.pollinations.ai/keys).

### Generated images

Ask for them in the chat, for example "Add a hero image made with Pollinations".

- AppBlips makes the images with your sign-in once the AI finishes, and pays for them from your Pollen. Most image models cost a small fraction of one Pollen per image.
- One request makes at most 12 new images.
- Each image is stored by Pollinations at a public web address, and that address goes into your project.
- When you [publish](/features/publishing) or [export](/features/exporting), AppBlips copies the images into the site, so they stay online as long as it does.
- Pollinations deletes an image nobody has viewed for 30 days. In a project you haven't opened for a month, an image can disappear. Ask the AI to make it again.

In the Browser Game studio, ask for images for things like the title screen, backgrounds or sprites. Everything else in a game is still drawn in code.

### AI features in your sites

AI features run in your visitors' browsers, so something has to pay each time a visitor uses one. Your own sign-in is never used for that. Under **AI features in your sites**, choose who pays, and the AI builds the features to match:

- **Visitors use their own Pollen (safer)** is the default. The feature shows a **Connect Pollinations** button. Each visitor signs in with their own Pollinations account and pays with their own Pollen. Nothing secret goes into your sites.
- **My key pays (the key goes in my sites' code)** lets visitors use AI features without an account. A **site key** you make pays: a separate Pollinations key with a small budget, shared by all your sites. Until you add one, the AI won't build AI features.

Ask for a feature in the chat, for example "Add an AI chatbot that answers visitors' questions, using Pollinations". If you change your choice later, sites you built earlier switch over when you publish or export them again.

#### Make and add a site key

<Warning>
  A site key has to be inside your sites so visitors' browsers can use it. AppBlips scrambles it so it isn't in plain sight, but that's not foolproof: anyone with a little know-how can find it and spend its Pollen. Only use a key made for this, with a small budget, so the most you can lose is that budget.
</Warning>

To make a safe key:

1. Go to [enter.pollinations.ai/keys](https://enter.pollinations.ai/keys) and sign in.
2. Create a new secret key. Name it something like "My AppBlips sites".
3. Set a small Pollen budget, for example 2. AppBlips won't accept a key without a budget.
4. Allow only the models your sites need: text for chat features, and image if visitors make pictures.
5. Set an end date if you can, so a leaked key stops working on its own.
6. Leave every account permission off.
7. Copy the key. It starts with `sk_`.

To add it to AppBlips:

1. In **Settings → Extensions → Pollinations**, choose **My key pays (the key goes in my sites' code)**.
2. Paste the key into the **Site key** box.
3. Click **Check key**. AppBlips asks Pollinations about the key, and refuses it if it has no budget or can manage your account.
4. Read any warnings, then tick **I understand that anyone can find this key in my sites and spend its Pollen.**

From then on, AI features in the preview and in everything you publish or export use this key. When its budget runs out or you delete it, the features show "AI features are unavailable right now." To stop using it, click **Remove key**. Sites you already published keep the key until you publish them again, so delete it on Pollinations' Keys page to stop those too.

## Google Sheets Backend

<Note>
  Google Sheets Backend is in beta. It may change, and some things may not work smoothly yet.
</Note>

On their own, the sites AppBlips builds keep everything a visitor does in that visitor's browser. An RSVP form can say "Thanks!", but you never see the reply, and a poll only counts the votes made on one device. Saving things where everyone, including you, can see them needs a **backend**: a computer somewhere else that stores information and shares it between visitors.

Google Sheets Backend turns a Google Sheet you own into a small, free backend. A short script you add to the Sheet runs on Google's servers, checks what visitors send, and saves it. Your sites can then:

- collect RSVPs, sign-ups and waitlists,
- receive private messages from visitors,
- run polls with live results from everyone,
- keep a shared high-score leaderboard for a game.

After setup, you never need to open the Sheet: your sites do the saving and the showing.

### Set up your Sheet

You need a Google account. Setup takes about five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**, then the **Google Sheets Backend** card. Click the switch next to **Enable Google Sheets Backend**. The setup steps appear under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new Sheet">
    Under **Set up your Sheet**, click **Click here**. Your web browser opens a new Google Sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the Sheet">
    Click **Untitled spreadsheet** at the top left, type a name you'll recognize, such as **My site data**, and press **Enter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Apps Script">
    In the Sheet, click the **Extensions** menu at the top (next to **Data** and **Tools**), then choose **Apps Script**. Apps Script opens in a new tab with a few lines of code already in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the AppBlips script">
    1. In AppBlips, click **Click to copy script**.
    2. In Apps Script, delete the code that's there and paste the script.
    3. Click the small **Save** icon above the code (a floppy disk), or press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘+S** on a Mac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy it as a web app">
    1. Click the blue **Deploy** button at the top right, then **New deployment**.
    2. Click the gear next to **Select type** and choose **Web app**.
    3. Set **Execute as** to **Me**.
    4. Set **Who has access** to **Anyone**.
    5. Click **Deploy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Google asks you to allow access. Click **Authorize access** and choose your Google account. If Google says it hasn't verified the app, click **Advanced**, then **Go to … (unsafe)**. Google shows that warning for every script it hasn't checked; this one is your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the web app address">
    Google shows a **Web app URL**. Copy it, go back to AppBlips and paste it into the **Web app URL** box. It starts with `https://script.google.com/` and ends with `/exec`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Test connection**. When it works, AppBlips says **Congratulations, you're all set!** The test doesn't add anything to your Sheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Setting **Who has access** to **Anyone** is what lets your sites reach the script. Don't share the web app address anywhere else.
</Warning>

### Use it

Ask for what you want in the chat, for example:

- "Add an RSVP form that saves each reply to my Google Sheet and shows how many people are coming."
- "Add a poll and show the live results, saved in my Google Sheet."
- "Add an online top 10 leaderboard saved in my Google Sheet."

More are under **Prompts to try** on the extension's page. One Sheet serves all your sites, and each site keeps its entries in its own tab, so they don't mix.

### Limits

Google Sheets Backend is a quick, free way to start, made for small sites. A dedicated backend such as Firebase or Supabase does more, but takes more setup.

- **Visitors can only add and read.** They can't edit or delete what they sent, and there are no visitor accounts.
- **No public posts from visitors.** Guestbooks, comments, reviews and message walls aren't supported yet, because there's no way to remove spam from them. If you ask for one, the AI stops before building anything and offers a private version instead: entries are saved for you, and the site only shows how many there are.
- **Private details stay private.** Email addresses and phone numbers are saved in your Sheet but never shown on your sites.
- **Basic protection only.** Anyone who can open your site can add entries. The script slows down floods of entries, but don't use it for passwords, payments or sensitive personal information.
- **Small sites only.** Google limits how much a free script can do each day. That's plenty for a party RSVP or a club site, but not for thousands of visitors an hour.
- **A little slow.** Each save or load takes a second or two.
- **Entries are real**, even from the preview. Anything you send while trying a site is saved and counted like a visitor's.

If **Test connection** says "Your script works, but there is a newer one", copy the script again in AppBlips, paste it over the old one in Apps Script, then click **Deploy → Manage deployments**, edit the deployment and choose **New version**.

## Pixabay

[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) is a library of millions of free photos. With a free Pixabay key, you can search it from inside AppBlips and add the photos you like to your project's [My images](/features/tools#my-images-beta). The AI then uses them like pictures you uploaded yourself.

An **API key** is a code that lets AppBlips search Pixabay for you. Every Pixabay account gets one for free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your key">
    1. Go to [pixabay.com](https://pixabay.com/) and click **Join** to create a free account, or **Log in**.
    2. Open [pixabay.com/api/docs](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/).
    3. Scroll to the **Parameters** section. Your key is shown next to **key**. Select it and copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to AppBlips">
    1. Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**, then the **Pixabay** card.
    2. Click the switch next to **Enable Pixabay**.
    3. Paste the key into the **API key** box and click **Save key**.
    4. AppBlips checks the key with Pixabay. When it works, the page says "Connected with the key ending in …".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find photos">
    1. Open the project you want photos for.
    2. In the search box, type what you're looking for, for example "mountain lake", and press **Enter**.
    3. Click each photo you want. A tick marks the ones you picked. Click **Load more** for more results.
    4. Click **Add to My images**. If My images was off, AppBlips turns it on so the AI can use the photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the AI to use them">
    Each photo gets a name made from its tags, such as `mountain-lake-sunset`. Mention that name in the chat, for example "use mountain-lake-sunset as the hero background".
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also search from **Settings → Tools → My images**: click **Find free photos on Pixabay** above your images.

- Pixabay photos are free to use, also commercially, under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Crediting the photographer isn't required, but it's appreciated. Each photo's note says who took it.
- Photos are copied into your project, so they keep working after you export or publish, even if Pixabay removes them.
- Pixabay limits how many searches a key can make each minute. If you see "Pixabay is busy", wait a minute and try again.
- To stop using your key, click **Remove key**.

## Tavily

<Note>The AI can also search the web with your AI provider's own search, with no key to add. See [Web search with your AI provider](/features/tools#web-search-with-your-ai-provider).</Note>

[Tavily](https://www.tavily.com/) is a search engine made for AI. With a Tavily key, the AI can search the web when it needs facts it doesn't know, such as a real business's address and opening hours, current news, or how to use a service, instead of guessing. Tavily's free plan includes 1,000 searches a month.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your key">
    1. Go to [app.tavily.com](https://app.tavily.com/) and create a free account, or sign in.
    2. On your Tavily dashboard, find your API key. It starts with `tvly-`.
    3. Click the copy button next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to AppBlips">
    1. Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**, then the **Tavily** card.
    2. Click the switch next to **Enable Tavily**.
    3. Paste the key into the **API key** box and click **Save key**.
    4. When the key works, the page says "Connected with the key ending in …".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build as usual">
    When a request needs real or up-to-date facts, the AI searches first, and the chat shows **Searching the web…** while it does. You can also ask directly, for example "search the web for the opening hours of Blue Door Café and add them to the contact section".
  </Step>
</Steps>

- Each search uses one Tavily credit. The AI makes at most 5 searches per request (3 before a project's first build), and none when it doesn't need to.
- Only the AI's search words go to Tavily, never your project's code.
- Search results can be wrong or out of date. Check important facts, like prices and opening hours, before you publish.
- If you see "Your Tavily key has run out of credits or reached its plan limit", check your usage at app.tavily.com. If Tavily stops accepting your key, the card shows **Needs key** again and the AI carries on without searching.

## Surge

Surge is the free hosting that **Publish** uses to put your sites online. Its card under **Settings → Extensions** lets you sign in or out ahead of time. See [Publish to the web](/features/publishing).

## Request an extension

Missing a service?

1. Click **Settings**, then **Extensions**.
2. Click **Request an extension** at the end of the cards.
3. Under **Service**, type the service's name. Under **What should it do?**, describe what you'd like.
4. Add **Your email** only if you'd like a reply.
5. Click **Send request**.

Your request goes to the AppBlips team through Web3Forms. Nothing about your projects is sent.

## Privacy

Your extension settings are saved with AppBlips on your device. AppBlips itself only contacts these services when it has to:

- **Web3Forms**, when you send an extension request.
- **Pollinations**, when you sign in, check a site key, or AppBlips makes images for you.
- **Google Sheets Backend**, when you click **Test connection**.
- **Pixabay**, when you save your key, search or add photos.
- **Tavily**, when you save your key or the AI searches the web.
- **Surge**, when you sign in, publish or unpublish.

The pages you build contact the services they use directly, in the preview and once they're online. For everything AppBlips connects to, see [the FAQ](/reference/faq).
