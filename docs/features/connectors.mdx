Extensions connect AppBlips to outside services. Once you connect one, the AI uses it whenever it builds or changes a project. Ask for "a contact form" and it emails you. Ask for "a buy button" and it opens your checkout.

AppBlips has no partnership or affiliate deal with these services. Each is a separate company with its own terms, prices and privacy policy.

## What you can connect

- **[Web3Forms](#web3forms):** emails you the entries from your forms.
- **[Umami](#umami)** and **[Plausible](#plausible):** count your visitors.
- **[tawk.to](#tawkto):** adds a live chat bubble.
- **[Square](#square):** adds buy, pay and donate buttons.
- **[Pollinations](#pollinations):** makes AI images and powers AI features like chatbots.
- **[Google Sheets Backend](#google-sheets-backend) (beta):** saves RSVPs, votes and scores in your own Google Sheet.
- **[Pixabay](#pixabay):** finds free stock photos.
- **[Tavily](#tavily):** lets the AI search the web for facts.
- **[Surge](#surge):** the free hosting that Publish uses.

SEO and My images are built into the app instead. See [Tools](/features/tools).

<Accordion title="Which studios use which extensions?">
  - **Responsive Website** and **Mobile & Web App** use all of them.
  - **Browser Game** uses Umami, Plausible, Pollinations, Google Sheets Backend and Tavily.
  - **Imported sites** (React + Vite projects) only use Tavily.
</Accordion>

## Set up an extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Extensions">
    Click **Settings**, then the **Extensions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Click its card. Click **How it works** at the top of the page if you want to read about it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click the switch next to **Enable** and the service's name, for example **Enable Square**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste what the service gives you">
    Paste the key, code or link into the box. The sections below say where to find it. A green message starting with **Connected.** means it looks right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save on their own. Each card shows its state: **Connected**, **Needs key** or **Needs sign-in**, or **Not connected**.

### Add an extension to a project you already built

Existing projects don't change on their own. To add a service to one:

1. Open the project, then click **Settings**, **Extensions** and the service's card.
2. Under **Prompts to try**, click **Copy** next to a request.
3. Close Settings, paste the request into the chat and send it.

Do the same if you change your key or link later.

### Where your keys go

Web3Forms, Umami, Plausible, tawk.to, Square and Google Sheets Backend only need a value that is meant to be public, like a form key or a payment link. It is written into your pages and into anything you publish or export.

Pollinations, Pixabay, Tavily and Surge use a sign-in or personal key. AppBlips never writes these into a page. The desktop app encrypts them with your computer's keychain. The browser version keeps them unencrypted in your own web browser, not on the server. Pixabay and Tavily keys go straight from your browser to those services. Surge and Pollinations don't accept browser requests, so their sign-ins pass through the AppBlips server, which doesn't store them.

## Web3Forms

**Use it for:** contact, signup and booking forms that email you their entries. It has a free plan.

1. Go to [web3forms.com](https://web3forms.com) and enter the email address for entries.
2. Web3Forms emails you an **access key**, a 36-character code.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Web3Forms and paste the key into the **Access key** box.

The form tells the visitor on the page whether sending worked. Forms also work in the preview, so a test entry lands in your inbox. The key can only send email to your own address, so it's safe in your site's code.

## Umami

**Use it for:** counting visitors without cookies. You see how many people came, which pages they viewed and where they came from. It has a free plan, and you can [run it yourself](https://github.com/umami-software/umami).

1. Sign up at [umami.is](https://umami.is) and add your website.
2. Open the website's settings and copy its tracking code.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Umami and paste it into **Tracking code or website ID**. On Umami Cloud you can paste just the 36-character website ID.

The AI adds the code to every page. Preview visits aren't counted, so stats start once the project is online.

## Plausible

**Use it for:** the same job as Umami, also without cookies. It's paid, with a free trial, and you can [run it yourself](https://github.com/plausible/community-edition).

1. Sign up at [plausible.io](https://plausible.io) and add your site, using the address it will have online.
2. Open the site's settings and copy everything in **Site installation**.
3. In AppBlips, turn on Plausible and paste it into the **Tracking code** box.

Preview visits aren't counted. Stats start once the project is online at the address you gave Plausible.

## tawk.to

**Use it for:** a live chat bubble in the corner of your pages. Visitors message you, and you reply from the tawk.to dashboard or phone app. It's free, with paid extras.

1. Sign up at [tawk.to](https://www.tawk.to) and create a property for your website.
2. Open **Administration**, then **Chat Widget**.
3. Copy everything in the **Widget Code** box, from `<!--Start of Tawk.to Script-->` to `<!--End of Tawk.to Script-->`.
4. In AppBlips, turn on tawk.to and paste it into the **Widget code** box.

The AI keeps the bottom-right corner clear for the bubble. Chat works in the preview too. tawk.to uses cookies, so your site may need a cookie notice where the law requires one.

## Square

**Use it for:** taking real payments. You create a **payment link** in Square for each thing you sell. The buy, pay and donate buttons on your site open that link. Square charges a fee on each payment.

1. Sign up at [squareup.com](https://squareup.com/payment-links) or sign in to your Square Dashboard.
2. Open **Payment links** (search "payment links" if you can't see it).
3. Create a link with a name and price.
4. Click **Copy link**. It starts with `https://square.link/u/`.
5. In AppBlips, turn on Square and paste it into the **Payment links** box.

To sell several things, put one link per line with the item's name first:

```text
T-shirt https://square.link/u/AbC123xy
Mug https://square.link/u/DeF456zw
```

Prices live in Square, so change them there. Payments are real even from the preview.

<Warning>
  Only paste payment links. AppBlips never needs your Square access token and refuses one if you paste it.
</Warning>

## Pollinations

**Use it for:** custom AI-generated images instead of stock photos, and AI features like a chatbot or writing helper. [Pollinations](https://github.com/pollinations/pollinations) runs on its own credits, called **Pollen**.

Using Pollinations through AppBlips costs 25% more than using it directly. The extra supports AppBlips.

### Sign in

1. Click **Settings**, **Extensions**, then the **Pollinations** card.
2. Click **Sign in with Pollinations**. A Pollinations page opens in your browser. If it doesn't, click **open it here**.
3. Sign in and approve AppBlips. Here you choose the most Pollen AppBlips may spend and how many days the sign-in lasts.
4. Back in AppBlips, check that it says **Signed in as** and your account name.
5. Click the switch next to **Enable Pollinations**.

When the days run out or the Pollen limit is spent, Pollinations turns off. Sign in again to continue. To end it sooner, click **Sign out**.

### Generated images

Ask in the chat, for example "Add a hero image made with Pollinations". Most images cost a small fraction of one Pollen.

- One request makes up to 12 new images.
- When you [publish](/features/publishing) or [export](/features/exporting), the images are copied into the site so they stay online with it.
- Pollinations deletes images nobody has viewed for 30 days. If one disappears from an old project, ask the AI to make it again.

### AI features in your sites

Something has to pay each time a visitor uses an AI feature. Under **AI features in your sites**, choose who:

- **Visitors use their own Pollen (safer)** is the default. Visitors click **Connect Pollinations** and pay with their own account. Nothing secret goes into your site.
- **My key pays (the key goes in my sites' code)** lets visitors use AI without an account. A separate **site key** you create pays for it. Until you add one, the AI won't build AI features.

Ask for a feature in the chat, for example "Add an AI chatbot that answers visitors' questions, using Pollinations".

<Accordion title="Make and add a site key (advanced)">
  The site key sits in your site's code so visitors' browsers can use it. AppBlips scrambles it, but a determined person can still find it and spend its Pollen. Use a key made just for this, with a small budget.

  1. Go to [enter.pollinations.ai/keys](https://enter.pollinations.ai/keys) and sign in.
  2. Create a new secret key, for example "My AppBlips sites".
  3. Set a small Pollen budget, such as 2. AppBlips won't accept a key without a budget.
  4. Allow only the models your sites need: text for chat, image if visitors make pictures.
  5. Set an end date if you can, and leave every account permission off.
  6. Copy the key. It starts with `sk_`.
  7. In AppBlips, choose **My key pays (the key goes in my sites' code)** and paste the key into **Site key**.
  8. Click **Check key**. AppBlips refuses a key with no budget or with account permissions.
  9. Tick **I understand that anyone can find this key in my sites and spend its Pollen.**

  If the budget runs out, the features show "AI features are unavailable right now." Click **Remove key** to stop using it. Sites you already published keep the key until you publish them again, so also delete it on Pollinations' Keys page.
</Accordion>

## Google Sheets Backend

<Note>
  Google Sheets Backend is in beta, so it may change.
</Note>

**Use it for:** saving what visitors do where you can see it: RSVPs, sign-ups, waitlists, private messages, polls with live results and game leaderboards. Normally a site keeps everything in each visitor's own browser, so you never see it. This extension saves entries in a Google Sheet you own, using a small free script. After setup you never need to open the Sheet.

### Set up your Sheet

You need a Google account. It takes about five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Settings**, **Extensions**, then the **Google Sheets Backend** card. Click the switch next to **Enable Google Sheets Backend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Sheet">
    Under **Set up your Sheet**, click **Click here**. A new Google Sheet opens. Give it a name you'll recognize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Apps Script">
    In the Sheet, click the **Extensions** menu, then **Apps Script**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the script">
    In AppBlips, click **Click to copy script**. In Apps Script, delete the existing code, paste the script and save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy it">
    Click **Deploy**, then **New deployment**. Click the gear next to **Select type** and choose **Web app**. Set **Execute as** to **Me** and **Who has access** to **Anyone**, then click **Deploy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Click **Authorize access** and choose your Google account. If Google says it hasn't verified the app, click **Advanced**, then **Go to … (unsafe)**. Google shows this for every unchecked script, and this one is your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the web app address">
    Copy the **Web app URL** (it ends in `/exec`) and paste it into the **Web app URL** box in AppBlips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Test connection**. You should see **Congratulations, you're all set!** The test adds nothing to your Sheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Setting **Who has access** to **Anyone** is what lets your sites reach the script. Don't share the web app address elsewhere.

### Use it

Ask in the chat, for example:

- "Add an RSVP form that saves each reply to my Google Sheet and shows how many people are coming."
- "Add a poll and show the live results, saved in my Google Sheet."
- "Add an online top 10 leaderboard saved in my Google Sheet."

One Sheet serves all your sites. Each site gets its own tab.

### Good to know

It's a quick, free start for small sites. Firebase or Supabase do more but take more setup.

- Visitors can only add and read entries. They can't edit or delete, and there are no accounts.
- Public posts from visitors (guestbooks, comments, reviews) aren't supported, because spam can't be removed. The AI offers a private version instead, where entries go to you and the site only shows a count.
- Emails and phone numbers are saved in your Sheet but never shown on your site.
- Protection is basic. Don't use it for passwords, payments or sensitive personal information.
- Google limits how much a free script can do each day, so it suits a party RSVP, not thousands of visitors an hour.
- Entries from the preview are real and are saved too.

If **Test connection** says "Your script works, but there is a newer one", copy the script again and paste it over the old one. Then click **Deploy**, **Manage deployments**, edit the deployment and choose **New version**.

## Pixabay

**Use it for:** free stock photos. You search [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) inside AppBlips and add photos to your project's [My images](/features/tools#my-images-beta), where the AI uses them like pictures you uploaded.

An **API key** is a code that lets AppBlips search on your behalf. Every Pixabay account gets one free.

1. Create a free account at [pixabay.com](https://pixabay.com/).
2. Open [pixabay.com/api/docs](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/). Your key is next to **key** in the **Parameters** section. Copy it.
3. In AppBlips, click **Settings**, **Extensions** and the **Pixabay** card.
4. Click the switch next to **Enable Pixabay**.
5. Paste the key into **API key** and click **Save key**. The page says "Connected with the key ending in …" when it works.

To add photos:

1. Open a project and type what you want in the search box, for example "mountain lake". Press **Enter**.
2. Click each photo you want. Click **Load more** for more.
3. Click **Add to My images**. This turns on My images if it was off.
4. Mention a photo's name in the chat, for example "use mountain-lake-sunset as the hero background".

You can also search from **Settings → Tools → My images** with **Find free photos on Pixabay**.

Photos are free to use, including commercially, under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Crediting is optional. Photos are copied into your project, so they keep working after you publish or export. If you see "Pixabay is busy", wait a minute. Click **Remove key** to disconnect.

## Tavily

**Use it for:** letting the AI look up real facts instead of guessing, such as a business's address and hours or current news. [Tavily](https://www.tavily.com/) is a search engine made for AI. Its free plan includes 1,000 searches a month.

Your AI provider's own web search needs no key. See [web search with your AI provider](/features/tools#web-search-with-your-ai-provider). If you connect Tavily, the AI uses it instead.

1. Create a free account at [app.tavily.com](https://app.tavily.com/).
2. Copy your API key from the dashboard. It starts with `tvly-`.
3. In AppBlips, click **Settings**, **Extensions** and the **Tavily** card.
4. Click the switch next to **Enable Tavily**.
5. Paste the key into **API key** and click **Save key**.

When a request needs real facts, the AI searches first and the chat shows **Searching the web…**. You can also ask directly: "Search the web for the opening hours of Blue Door Café and add them to the contact section."

- Each search uses one Tavily credit. The AI makes at most 5 searches per request.
- Only the AI's search words go to Tavily, never your project's code.
- Results can be wrong or out of date. Check prices and hours before you publish.
- If Tavily runs out of credits, check your usage at app.tavily.com. If it rejects your key, the card shows **Needs key** and the AI carries on without searching.

## Surge

Surge is the free hosting that **Publish** uses to put your sites online. Its card under **Settings → Extensions** lets you sign in or out ahead of time. See [Publish to the web](/features/publishing).

## Request an extension

Missing a service? Click **Settings**, **Extensions**, then **Request an extension** at the end of the cards. Type the **Service** and **What should it do?**, add **Your email** if you want a reply, and click **Send request**. Nothing about your projects is sent.

## Privacy

Your extension settings are saved on your device. AppBlips only contacts a service when it has to: when you save a key, sign in, test a connection, search, publish, or send a request. The pages you build contact their services directly. For everything AppBlips connects to, see [the FAQ](/reference/faq).
