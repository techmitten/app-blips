Tools are features built into AppBlips that the AI uses in your projects. Unlike [extensions](/features/connectors), they don't connect to an outside service, so there's nothing to sign up for.

- **[SEO](#seo):** helps search engines find your site.
- **[My images](#my-images-beta) (beta):** lets the AI use your own photos and logos.
- **[Web search](#web-search-with-your-ai-provider):** lets the AI look up facts using your AI provider's search.

## Turn on a tool

1. Click **Settings**, then the **Tools** tab.
2. Click the tool's card. Click **How it works** at the top if you want to read about it first.
3. Click the switch next to **Enable** and the tool's name, for example **Enable SEO**.

Changes save on their own. To use a tool in a project you already built, click **Copy** next to a request under **Prompts to try**, close Settings and paste it into the chat.

## SEO

SEO (search engine optimization) helps Google and other search engines understand your site and show it to the right people. You answer a few questions, and the AI adds good SEO to your pages.

SEO is set per project, so open the project first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    With a project open, click **Settings**, **Tools**, then the **SEO** card, and click **Enable SEO**. The questions open in their own window. Later, click **Review answers** to see them again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Click **Next** to move on, **Back** to go back, or **Skip** to leave a page out. Only the first page is required.

    - **Tell us about your site:** its name, what it does and its website address. Leave the address blank if you don't have one yet.
    - **Who is it for?** Your visitors, the kind of site and where you're based.
    - **How should people find you?** Search phrases, your language or country, and **Let search engines list this site**. Turn that off while the site is a draft.
    - **When someone shares your link:** your other profiles, such as Instagram, and a preview picture from [My images](#my-images-beta).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and save">
    Click **Edit** next to a group to change it. Then choose **Send now** to have the AI apply your answers right away, or **Maybe later** to save them for your next request. You can also click **Apply now** on the SEO page whenever you're ready.

    If nothing is built yet, click **Save**. The AI uses your answers when it builds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The AI then adds titles and descriptions, share previews, clear headings, image descriptions and structured data about your business.

### Scan your existing site

If you type a website address, AppBlips asks whether it's OK to scan your site. Click **Yes, scan it** and AppBlips reads that one page's title, description and headings, once, so the AI can match what it already says. Click **No thanks** and nothing is read. Afterwards you can click **Scan again** or **Forget it**. Only public websites can be scanned.

### Sitemap and robots files

If you gave a website address, [exporting](/features/exporting) and [publishing](/features/publishing) also add two files:

- `sitemap.xml` lists your pages for search engines.
- `robots.txt` points them to the sitemap, or asks them to stay away if you turned off **Let search engines list this site**.

An export that includes them downloads as a `.zip`, even for a one-page site.

SEO can't promise a ranking. Imported sites don't use it.

## My images (beta)

My images lets you add your own photos and logos to a project, so the AI builds with your pictures instead of stock or generated ones. It's in beta, so it may change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Settings**, **Tools**, then the **My images** card, and click **Enable My images**. It starts off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add pictures">
    With a project open, click **Upload images**, or drag and drop or paste pictures onto the page. Use JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or SVG, up to 25 per project. Big photos are resized to 2,000 pixels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe them">
    Each image gets a short name you can change. Add a note about what it shows or where it should go, such as "our team at the summer party".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the AI to use them">
    Mention an image's name in the chat, for example "put team-photo in the hero section". Click **Copy name** to copy it. To use them all, say "use all my images in a gallery".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the match">
    Before building, the AI shows which images it matched. Click **Yes, use it** to go ahead, or type the right image's name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **The AI never sees the pictures.** It only gets each image's name, size and note.
- **Hide an image** with the eye icon. It's marked **Hidden from the AI**, but pages that already show it keep showing it.
- **Free stock photos:** with the [Pixabay](/features/connectors#pixabay) extension set up, click **Find free photos on Pixabay**.
- **Images belong to one project.** They aren't part of its version history or a JSON backup.
- **Exports and published sites** include only the images your pages use.
- Imported sites don't use My images.

## Web search with your AI provider

Each AI provider has its own web search tool: **DeepSeek Web Search**, **Claude Web Search**, **OpenAI Web Search**, **Gemini Google Search**, **Grok Web Search** and **OpenRouter Web Search**. They need no extra key. The AI uses the same key that writes your projects.

1. Open **Settings → Tools**. The tool for your provider says **In use**. AppBlips turns it on for you.
2. A globe button appears under the chat box. It's red while search is on. Click it to turn search off or on for your prompts.
3. Build as usual. When a request needs up-to-date facts, the AI searches first and the chat shows **Searching the web…**.

- Your provider charges for each search on top of normal usage. Check your provider's pricing page for the current price.
- The AI searches only when a request needs it: up to 3 times per request (DeepSeek: up to 5 searches and 3 page reads).
- Your model must support web search: Claude 4.5 and later, GPT-5 models, Gemini 2.5 and later, and Grok 4 and later do. If yours doesn't, the chat says so and the AI carries on without searching. A Claude organization admin can also switch search off in the Claude Console.
- To remove the globe, switch off the tool that's in use.
- If the [Tavily](/features/connectors#tavily) extension is connected, the AI uses Tavily instead.
- It doesn't work with a custom endpoint.

## Privacy

SEO uses no outside service. It only reads your website after you say yes. Your answers are saved with the project and go only to your AI provider. My images keeps your pictures with the project on your device. For everything AppBlips connects to, see [the FAQ](/reference/faq).
