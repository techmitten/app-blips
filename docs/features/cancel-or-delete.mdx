You can clear out your work at three levels: delete one project, delete all projects, or reset AppBlips completely.

<Tip>
  Want a copy first? Open **Settings → Data** and click **Export** next to **Export all apps**. It saves every project, with its versions, as one backup file you can import later.
</Tip>

## Delete one project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your projects">
    Click **Projects** in the header, or **My projects** on the studio picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the project">
    Scroll to it, or type part of its name in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the trash button">
    It's on the project's row. Its tooltip says **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete** in the **Delete project** box. Click **Cancel** to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The project and all its versions are removed.

## Delete all projects

This keeps your settings, AI provider and extensions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then **Danger Zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete them all">
    Next to **Delete all saved apps**, click **Delete all**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete all** in the **Delete all saved apps?** box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset AppBlips

A reset puts AppBlips back to how it was on first install. It removes:

- every project and its version history
- all your settings
- your AI provider, model and API key
- your sign-ins and keys for services such as Surge, Pollinations, Pixabay and Tavily

AppBlips then restarts and walks you through setup again.

<Warning>
  A reset can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then **Danger Zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the reset">
    Next to **Reset app to factory defaults**, click **Factory Reset**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Reset App** in the **Factory Reset?** box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete your sign-in account

If you use the free AI and signed in to it, **Danger Zone** also shows **Delete your account**, with a **Delete account** button. It permanently deletes that sign-in account. Your projects are separate; delete them as described above.

## Can I get a deleted project back?

- **Desktop app:** a deleted project's folder goes to your Recycle Bin (Windows) or Trash (Linux), so you can restore it from there. If your computer has no Trash, it is deleted straight away.
- **Browser version:** it's gone for good.

In the browser version, projects and keys live only in that browser. Clearing the browser's site data for AppBlips deletes them too, like a reset. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.
