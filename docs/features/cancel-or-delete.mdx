---

You can remove your work at three levels:

- **Delete one project** you no longer need.
- **Delete all projects** but keep your settings and AI setup.
- **Reset AppBlips** to how it was on the first day: no projects, no settings, no saved keys.

<Tip>
  Want to keep a copy first? Open **Settings → Data** and click **Export** next to **Export all apps**. It downloads every project, with its versions, as one backup file you can import again later.
</Tip>

## Delete one project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your projects">
    Click **Projects** in the header, or **My projects** on the studio picker. The **Your projects** list opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the project">
    Scroll to it, or type part of its name in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it">
    Click the trash key on its row (its tooltip says **Delete**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A box asks **Delete project**, naming the project. Click **Delete**. To keep it, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The project and all of its versions are removed. If it was the project you had open, the workspace is cleared.

## Delete all projects

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then click **Danger Zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete them all">
    Next to **Delete all saved apps**, click **Delete all**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A box titled **Delete all saved apps?** says how many will go. Click **Delete all**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every project and its version history is removed. Your settings, your AI provider and your extensions stay as they are.

## Reset AppBlips

A reset puts AppBlips back to how it was when you first installed it.

<Warning>
  A reset removes everything at once and can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then click **Danger Zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the reset">
    Next to **Reset app to factory defaults**, click **Factory Reset**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    A box titled **Factory Reset?** opens. Click **Reset App**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A reset removes:

- Every project and its version history.
- All your settings.
- Your AI provider, model and API key.
- Your sign-ins and keys for outside services, such as Surge (for publishing), Pollinations, Pixabay and Tavily.

AppBlips then restarts and walks you through the first-time setup again.

## Where deleted projects go

- **Desktop app:** each project is a folder in your projects folder. A deleted project's folder goes to your computer's Recycle Bin (Windows) or Trash (Linux), so you can get it back from there until you empty it. If your computer has no Trash, the folder is deleted straight away.
- **Browser version:** projects are kept in your web browser's storage. A deleted project is gone for good.

In the browser version, your projects and keys live only in the browser you use AppBlips in. Clearing that browser's site data for AppBlips (for example with "Clear browsing data") deletes them too, just like a reset. Export a backup from **Settings → Data** first if you want to keep them.
