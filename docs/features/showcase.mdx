The Showcase is a gallery of real projects built with AppBlips, each from a single prompt. Open any of them inside the app, and send in your own to be featured.

## Browse the Showcase

1. Go to the first screen, where you choose what to build. If you're in a project, click **New** in the header first.
2. Click **Explore**, below the studios.
3. Click **Websites** or **Games** to filter. **All** shows everything again.
4. Click a project (**Explore website** or **Play game**) to open it full size. Games play with keyboard, mouse or touch.

While a project is open, **Open in browser** opens it in your web browser. The back arrow or Escape returns to the gallery.

The examples come with AppBlips, so most open without internet. Some load pictures or code from the web and need a connection. If one can't load, AppBlips says `This project couldn't be loaded.` and links to open it in your browser. Showcase projects run in the same isolated preview as your own apps, so they can't reach your projects, settings or keys.

## Submit your project

Your project needs to be online at a public address. [Publish to the web](/features/publishing) does that for free.

1. In the Showcase, click **Submit yours** or the **Submit your project** card at the end of the gallery. Right after publishing, you can click **Submit it to the showcase** in the Publish window to fill in the name, link and type for you.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Your name:** how you'd like to be credited.
   - **Your email:** so the team can reply. It's never shown publicly.
   - **Project name**
   - **Published link:** for example `https://my-project.surge.sh`.
   - **It's a…:** the kind of project.
   - **Tell us about it** (optional): what it does and the prompt you started from.
3. Tick the box confirming you made the project and that AppBlips may show it, its name and a screenshot, credited to you.
4. Click **Submit project**.

## What happens next

The AppBlips team reviews every submission by hand, and nothing appears until it's approved. If yours is picked, you'll get an email before it goes live. It then appears in the Showcase and on [appblips.com](https://www.appblips.com/showcase).

The form is sent through Web3Forms, an email service. Only what you type in the form is sent. You can send one submission a minute.
