The Showcase is a gallery of real projects built with AppBlips, each from a single prompt. You can open any of them inside AppBlips: scroll through a website or play a game. It's a good place to get ideas, and you can send in your own project to be featured.

## Browse the Showcase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the studio picker">
    It's the first screen, where you choose what to build. If you're in a project, click **New** in the header first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Explore">
    Click **Explore** (See what others built with AppBlips), below the studios.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter, if you like">
    Click **Websites** or **Games** at the top to see only one kind. **All** shows everything again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a project">
    Click a project (**Explore website** or **Play game**) to open it full size. Websites scroll and click as normal. Games play with your keyboard, mouse or touch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While a project is open:

- **Open in browser** opens it in your web browser instead.
- The back arrow, or Escape, takes you back to the gallery.

The example projects come with AppBlips, so they open without an internet connection. Some of them load pictures or code from the web, so those parts need one. If a project can't load, AppBlips says `This project couldn't be loaded.` with a link to open it in your browser.

Each project runs in the same walled-off preview as your own apps, so it can't reach your projects, your settings or your keys.

## Submit your project

To be featured, your project needs to be online at a public address that anyone can open. [Publish to the web](/features/publishing) puts it online for free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the Showcase, click **Submit yours** (top right) or the **Submit your project** card at the end of the gallery.

    Just published? You can also click **Submit it to the showcase** in the Publish window. The form then opens with your project's name, link and type already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    - **Your name:** how you'd like to be credited.
    - **Your email:** so the AppBlips team can reply. It's never shown publicly.
    - **Project name**
    - **Published link:** the address where it's online, such as `https://my-project.surge.sh`.
    - **It's a…:** choose what kind of project it is.
    - **Tell us about it** (optional): what it does, and the prompt you started from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give permission">
    Tick the box that says you made the project and that AppBlips may show it, its name and a screenshot in the Showcase, credited to your name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Submit project**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

The AppBlips team looks at every submission by hand. Nothing appears in the Showcase until it's approved. If your project is picked, you'll get an email before it goes live. It's then added to the Showcase inside AppBlips and on [appblips.com](https://www.appblips.com/showcase).

The form is sent through Web3Forms, an email service. Only what you type in the form is sent, nothing else about your projects. You can send one submission a minute.
