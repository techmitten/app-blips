AppBlips builds apps, websites and games from a description. Tell it what you want in plain words, an AI writes the code, and your project appears in a live preview next to the chat.

You never have to look at code. If you want to, you can read it, edit it or take it with you.

## What you can build

Pick one of three studios:

- **Mobile & Web App**: tools that work on any device, like a budget tracker, a quiz or a timer.
- **Responsive Website**: multi-page sites that fit every screen, like a portfolio or an event page.
- **Browser Game**: 2D and 3D games with keyboard and touch controls.

The start screen also has:

- **My projects**: pick up where you left off.
- **Import a site**: keep editing an HTML page, a website `.zip` or a React + Vite project. See [Import a site](/features/importing-sites).
- **Explore**: try projects other people built, and share yours. See [Showcase](/features/showcase).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Type your idea. You can also speak it, or attach a screenshot or sketch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it build">
    The AI writes the code and you watch it appear. Then the project runs in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for changes">
    Say what you'd like different. Every change is saved as a version, so you can undo it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it">
    [Download your project](/features/exporting) or [publish it to the web](/features/publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you need

AppBlips is free and has no account of its own. It needs an AI to write the code, and you get one in one of two ways:

- **Your own API key.** An API key is a password-like code from an AI company such as Anthropic or OpenAI. The company bills you for what you use. See [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers).
- **Free AI.** Some copies, including [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com), include free AI. Choose **Free (no key needed)**. It's shared and limited, and may ask you to sign in with Google or GitHub.
