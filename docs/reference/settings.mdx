Open **Settings** from the header to change how AppBlips looks and works. Changes save on your device straight away. Click **Done** to close.

---

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your computer). Default: **Dark**.
- **Build pane font**: text size for the starter ideas, chat and prompt box. Choose **Small**, **Default**, **Large** or **XL**. Default: **Small**.
- **Build pane position**: put the build pane on the **Left** or **Right** of the preview. It applies on wider screens. Default: **Left**.

## Workspace

- **Code view**: shows the **Code** tab in the preview toolbar, where you can read and edit your app's code. Default: on. See [Edit the code yourself](/features/live-preview#edit-the-code-yourself).
- **Auto Follow Code**: while an app is being written, the **Code** tab scrolls to the newest line. Default: on.
- **Code autocomplete**: suggests words as you edit code. The suggestions come from your own code and common keywords, never from the AI. Default: on.
- **Live code preview**: shows the code being written in the preview pane during a build. Default: on.
- **Skip splash screen**: skips the intro animation when AppBlips opens. Default: off.
- **Check for updates**: checks GitHub for new versions when AppBlips starts and every hour after that. It also shows your version, and **Check now** checks straight away. Default: on. How an update arrives depends on how you installed AppBlips:
  - **AppImage**: downloads in the background, then offers **Restart to update**.
  - **`.deb`**: the notice gives you a download link and the command to install it.
  - **Snap**: App Center updates it; AppBlips tells you when a new version is out.
  - **Microsoft Store**: the Store updates it.
- **Replay onboarding**: **Replay** walks you through the welcome screens and AI setup again. Your projects stay as they are.

## AI

### AI coding provider

The AI service that builds, edits and answers questions for you. See [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers).

1. Choose a **Provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek or OpenRouter.
2. Paste your **API key**. A link under the box opens the provider's key page.
3. Pick a **Model**, or type a model ID.
4. Click **Test connection**, then **Save**.

A few things to know:

- **Free (no key needed)** appears first in the list when the copy of AppBlips you use offers it, as [web.appblips.com](/quickstart-browser) does. It may ask you to sign in with Google or GitHub, and it has usage limits.
- Once saved, the key box says **Saved (encrypted) — type to replace** in the desktop app, or just **Saved — type to replace** in the browser version and on Linux without a keychain, and the key is never shown again. To change it, click the box and then **Replace key**.
- **Refresh models** reloads the model list. If it can't load, type the model ID yourself.
- **Clear** removes the saved provider, model and key.
- If you leave with unsaved changes, AppBlips asks **Save your AI provider?**

Your key stays on your computer: in the system keychain on the desktop app, or in your browser on the browser version. See [Security](/self-hosting/security#your-api-key).

### Output tokens

A token is a small piece of text, about three-quarters of a word. These two fields cap how long one AI reply can be.

- **Output tokens: building**: the longest reply when building, editing or fixing. Default: 64,000, enough for a large app. **Use default** clears your value.
- **Output tokens: Ask**: the longest reply in Ask mode. Default: whatever your provider uses (64,000 on Anthropic, which needs a limit). **Use provider default** clears your value.

If the server sets its own limit for the Free option (`APPBLIPS_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS`), these fields are hidden while you use Free. They appear again when you switch to your own provider and key.

### Reasoning

How much the AI thinks before it writes. Lower levels are faster and cheaper; higher levels suit complex apps but take longer and use more tokens. Default: **Medium**.

The list only shows levels your provider and model support, and marks the model's own default. If your chosen level isn't available, AppBlips uses the closest one. Quick replies, such as clarifying questions and Ask mode, always run with reasoning off.

### Other AI settings

- **Clarifying questions**: the AI asks 2 to 3 multiple-choice questions before the first build. Default: on.
- **Suggested prompts**: after each build, suggests next prompts above the chat box. Uses a few extra tokens. Default: off.
- **Smart game engine**: in the Game studio, the AI picks an engine such as Phaser or Three.js for each game. An engine you name always wins. Default: on. See [Game engines](/features/building-apps#game-engines).
- **Smart website add-ons**: in the Website studio, the AI adds libraries a site needs, such as GSAP or Chart.js. Off means plain JavaScript only. Default: on. See [Website add-ons](/features/building-apps#website-add-ons).

## Extensions

Extensions connect outside services to your projects. Each card shows **Connected**, **Needs key**, **Needs sign-in** or **Not connected**. Click a card to set it up. Every extension is off until you turn it on. See [Extensions](/features/connectors) for setup steps.

- **Web3Forms**: emails you what visitors type into your forms.
- **Umami** and **Plausible**: count visitors to your sites.
- **tawk.to**: adds a live chat bubble.
- **Square**: takes payments through your own Square checkout.
- **Pollinations**: AI images and AI features in your sites.
- **Google Sheets Backend** (beta): saves what visitors send in a Google Sheet you own.
- **Pixabay**: search free stock photos and add them to your project.
- **Tavily**: lets the AI search the web while it builds.
- **Surge**: the free hosting account [Publish](/features/publishing) uses. Click it to sign in or out.

Missing a service? Click **Request an extension**.

## Tools

Tools are built in and need no outside account. Each has an **Enable** switch. See [Tools](/features/tools).

- **SEO**: helps people find your site on Google. It works per project, so open a project first.
- **My images** (beta): lets the AI use photos and logos you upload to the open project.
- **Web search for your provider**: one tool per provider, named **DeepSeek Web Search**, **Claude Web Search**, **OpenAI Web Search**, **Gemini Google Search**, **Grok Web Search** and **OpenRouter Web Search**. The one for the provider you've chosen in **Settings → AI** adds a globe button under the chat box, so the AI can search the web for facts when you build. Each search is billed to your provider key. Switch the tool off to remove the globe. In the browser version, there's no globe with Free AI. If the Tavily extension is connected, it's used for searching instead.

## Data

- **Projects folder** (desktop app): where your projects are saved. **Open** shows the folder; **Change…** moves your projects to another one. The browser version saves projects in your browser.
- **Export all apps**: downloads every saved app, with its history, as one backup file. Imported sites aren't included; export each from its own project as a `.zip`. Images from **My images** aren't included either.
- **Import apps**: restores apps from a backup file. An app you already have is replaced only if the backup's copy is newer. The desktop and browser versions can import each other's backups.

See [Back up or move your projects](/quickstart-desktop#back-up-or-move-your-projects).

## About

Shows your version and whether you're on the **Desktop app** or **Browser version**. It also has links to the AppBlips **Website**, the **Source code**, **Report a bug**, **Send feedback** and **Donate**. Links that leave AppBlips ask first. **Legal** has the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Surge terms. **Licenses** lists the open source code AppBlips uses.

## Danger Zone

<Warning>
  These actions are permanent.
</Warning>

- **Delete your account**: only shown when you're signed in to Free AI. Deletes your sign-in account. Apps saved in this browser stay.
- **Delete all saved apps**: removes every saved app and its history.
- **Reset app to factory defaults**: click **Factory Reset**, then **Reset App**. Deletes all apps, history, settings and API keys, signs you out of connected services and restarts AppBlips.
