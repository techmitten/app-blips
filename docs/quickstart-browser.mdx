The quickest way to try AppBlips is in your web browser at [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com). There's nothing to install and no account to create, and it comes with free AI so you can start building straight away.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AppBlips">
    Go to [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com) in any modern browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell AppBlips about you">
    On **First, what should we call you?**, type your name or a nickname and click **Continue**. Then pick an avatar for your chat messages and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the free AI">
    The next screen, **AI is included, free.**, explains the free option. Click **Continue**. On **Choose the AI that builds with you.**, **Free (no key needed)** is already selected. Click **Start building**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build something">
    Click a studio, such as **Mobile & Web App**, **Responsive Website** or **Browser Game**. Describe what you want, for example "a quiz about the planets with a score at the end", and press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free AI or your own key

**Free (no key needed)** uses AI that's been set up for everyone using web.appblips.com. It's a great way to try AppBlips, but because it's shared, it has usage limits and can be slower when many people are building. If you reach a limit, AppBlips tells you when you can continue.

For your own choice of provider and model, without the shared limits, use your own API key:

1. Click **Settings** (the gear icon at the top of the screen) and open the **AI** tab.
2. Under **AI coding provider**, choose your provider from the **Provider** list.
3. Paste your key in the API key box, and choose a **Model**.
4. Click **Save**.

Don't have a key yet? [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers) explains what a key is and how to get one. To go back to the free AI, choose **Free (no key needed)** in the same list and click **Save**.

## Things to know about the browser version

- **Your projects stay in this browser.** They're saved in your browser's storage on this device, not on AppBlips' server. Another browser, another device or a private window starts empty.
- **Clearing your browser's data deletes them.** Clearing site data, cookies or history for web.appblips.com removes your projects. Make a backup now and then: open **Settings → Data** and click **Export** next to **Export all apps**. To restore it, click **Import…** next to **Import apps**.
- **Your API key stays in this browser too.** AppBlips sends it to its server with each request so the request can reach your AI provider. The server doesn't store it, and it's never put into your projects.
- **Want more?** The [desktop app](/quickstart-desktop) saves projects as folders on your computer and protects your key with your computer's keychain. You can bring your projects with you: export a backup here and import it in the desktop app.
