Open [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com) and start building in a few minutes. There's nothing to install. You need an API key from an AI provider such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. See [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers) if you don't have one yet.

## Start building

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AppBlips">
    Go to [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com) in a modern browser, then click **Get started** on the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and accept the terms">
    Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click **I agree**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name and pick an avatar">
    Type a name or nickname, click **Continue**, choose an avatar and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read how saving works">
    One screen explains that projects are stored in your browser. Click **I understand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI provider">
    On **Choose the AI that builds with you.**, choose your **Provider**, paste your **API key** and choose a **Model**. Click **Test connection** to check them if you like, then click **Start building**. No key yet? **Get a key** opens your provider's key page, or click **Skip for now** and add one later in **Settings → AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build something">
    AppBlips opens in the App studio. For a website or a game, click the **Website studio** or **Game studio** icon at the bottom of the prompt box. Describe what you want, such as "a quiz about the planets with a score at the end", and press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your provider or key

1. Click **Settings** and open the **AI** tab.
2. Choose a **Provider**.
3. Paste your API key and choose a **Model**.
4. Click **Save**.

Your provider bills you for what you use. See [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers) to compare them.

## Good to know

- **Your projects live in this browser.** A different browser, device or private window starts empty.
- **Clearing site data deletes them.** Back up often: open **Settings → Data** and click **Export** next to **Export all projects**. To restore, click **Import…** next to **Import projects**.
- **Your API key stays in this browser.** Your browser sends it straight to your AI provider. It never goes to AppBlips' server.
- **Published sites stay online** even if you clear your browser data.

Want projects as folders on your computer? Use the [desktop app](/quickstart-desktop) and import your backup there.
