Open [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com) and start building in a few minutes. There's nothing to install, and it includes free AI.

## Start building

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AppBlips">
    Go to [web.appblips.com](https://web.appblips.com) in a modern browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and accept the terms">
    Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click **I agree**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name and pick an avatar">
    Type a name or nickname, click **Continue**, choose an avatar and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read how saving works">
    One screen explains that projects are stored in your browser. Click **I understand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the free AI">
    Click **Continue** on the free AI screen. On **Choose the AI that builds with you.**, **Free (no key needed)** is already selected. Click **Start building**. You may be asked to sign in with Google or GitHub the first time you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build something">
    Click **Mobile & Web App**, **Responsive Website** or **Browser Game**. Describe what you want, such as "a quiz about the planets with a score at the end", and press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use your own API key

Free AI is shared, so it has limits, and it can't read images or search the web. For no shared limits, your choice of provider and model, image and screenshot attachments, and web search, use your own key:

1. Click **Settings** and open the **AI** tab.
2. Choose a **Provider**.
3. Paste your API key and choose a **Model**.
4. Click **Save**.

You don't have a key yet? See [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers). To go back to free AI, choose **Free (no key needed)** and save.

## Good to know

- **Your projects live in this browser.** A different browser, device or private window starts empty.
- **Clearing site data deletes them.** Back up often: open **Settings → Data** and click **Export** next to **Export all apps**. To restore, click **Import…** next to **Import apps**.
- **Your API key stays in this browser.** Your browser sends it straight to your AI provider. It never goes to AppBlips' server.
- **Published sites stay online** even if you clear your browser data.

Want projects as folders on your computer? Use the [desktop app](/quickstart-desktop) and import your backup there.
