The desktop app saves each project as a folder on your computer and keeps your API key in your computer's keychain. There's no account to create.

## Install

### Windows

1. Open [AppBlips in the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9nk9ss4v2n1k).
2. Click **Get** (or **Install**).
3. Open AppBlips from the Start menu.

You can also download `AppBlips-Windows-Installer.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/TechMitten/app-blips/releases/latest). It's Microsoft's small installer, and it installs the same Store version.

### Linux

Pick one:

- **Ubuntu App Center (snap).** Search for AppBlips, or run `sudo snap install appblips --stable`.
- **`.deb`** for Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint and similar. Download it from the [latest release](https://github.com/TechMitten/app-blips/releases/latest), then run `sudo apt install ./AppBlips-*.deb` in the download folder.
- **AppImage** for other systems. Download it from the same page, run `chmod +x AppBlips-*.AppImage`, then start it with `./AppBlips-*.AppImage`.

### macOS

There's no Mac installer. Use [Pinokio](https://pinokio.co), a free app that installs and runs projects like AppBlips:

1. Install Pinokio.
2. Open the [AppBlips page in Pinokio](https://pinokio.co/apps/github-com-techmitten-app-blips-pinokio) and download it.
3. Click **Install**, then **Start**, then **Open AppBlips**.

This runs the browser version, so it behaves like the [browser version](/quickstart-browser#good-to-know): projects and key stay in your web browser. Click **Update** in Pinokio to get new versions. You can also [run from source](/quickstart-source).

## Set up AppBlips

The first launch asks a few questions and takes about a minute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Read the introduction and click **Get started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click **I agree**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your name">
    Type a name or nickname. It appears next to your chat messages. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avatar and projects folder">
    Pick an avatar. Under **Save projects in**, the default folder is fine for most people. Click **Change** to pick another. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI">
    On **Choose the AI that builds with you.**:

    1. Choose a **Provider** (Anthropic is marked recommended).
    2. Paste your **API key**. No key yet? Click **Get a key**, or see [Choose an AI provider](/ai-providers).
    3. Choose a **Model**. The list loads from your provider once your key is in.
    4. Optional: click **Test connection** to check it works.
    5. Click **Start building**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change your name, avatar or folder later, open **Settings → Workspace** and click **Replay** next to **Replay onboarding**. Your projects and AI setup are kept.

Your key is encrypted by your computer's keychain and never goes into your projects. On Linux without a keychain, the protection is weak. AppBlips warns you and suggests installing one such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet.

## Build your first project

1. AppBlips opens in the App studio. For a website or a game, click the **Website studio** or **Game studio** icon at the bottom of the prompt box.
2. Describe what you want, for example "a habit tracker with a checkbox for each day".
3. Press Enter.

To change it, describe the change and press Enter again. See [Build apps](/features/building-apps) for tips.

## Where your projects are saved

Each project is a folder:

- **Windows:** `C:\Users\<your name>\AppData\Roaming\AppBlips\Projects`
- **Linux:** `~/.config/AppBlips/Projects`
- **Snap:** `~/snap/appblips/common/Projects` for new installs. Existing projects stay where they were.

To open the folder, go to **Settings → Data**, find **Projects folder** and click **Open**. Click **Change…** to move it somewhere else. AppBlips offers to move your existing projects.

Each folder holds the whole project, including versions and chat, plus a `site` folder with plain `.html` copies of the pages. You can share those files, but editing them doesn't change the project in AppBlips. Deleting a project moves its folder to your trash.

<Warning>
  Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version can delete the default projects folder. Back up first, or pick your own folder with **Change…**.
</Warning>

## Back up or move your projects

1. Open **Settings → Data**.
2. Next to **Export all projects**, click **Export**. You get one backup file.
3. To restore it, click **Import…** next to **Import projects** and choose the file. This works on another computer, and with backups from the browser version.

Imported React + Vite sites aren't in the backup. Download each as a `.zip` instead. See [Import a site](/features/importing-sites).

## Updates

Updates never touch your projects or settings. To check now, open **Settings → Workspace** and click **Check now** next to **Check for updates**. The switch beside it turns automatic checks off. Checks contact GitHub, which sees your IP address.

- **Microsoft Store:** the Store updates the app. The notice has an **Open Microsoft Store** button.
- **Snap:** App Center updates it. The notice has an **Open App Center** button. Close AppBlips before updating.
- **AppImage:** new versions download in the background. Click **Restart to update** when it appears, or **Later** to install when you next close the app.
- **`.deb`:** AppBlips tells you when a version is out and shows a command to copy. It looks like `sudo apt install ~/Downloads/AppBlips-<version>.deb`, after you download the file.
